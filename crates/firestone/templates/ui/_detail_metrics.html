Every number and every sparkline point is written by app.js from two samples of GET /v1/machines/{name}/metrics, because those counters are cumulative and a rate is a difference, not a reading. Until two samples exist the strip says so rather than drawing a line through one point. The strip deliberately sits outside #fs-detail-live: that region is repolled and swapped every five seconds, and a swap would throw away the ring buffer the sparklines are drawn from. #}
